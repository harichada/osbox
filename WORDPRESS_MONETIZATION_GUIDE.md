## 🔧 Shortcode Usage Guide

### Email Signup Form

```
[email_signup
  title="Get Free AI Guide"
  description="Join 10,000+ developers"
  button="Download Now"
  placeholder="Your email address"
]
```

**Where to Use**:
- Sidebar widgets
- End of blog posts
- Landing pages
- Pop-ups (with plugin)

### CTA Box

```
[cta_box
  title="Need Help Implementing AI?"
  button_text="Book Free Call"
  button_url="/contact"
  style="gradient"
]
Custom text goes here describing the offer
[/cta_box]
```

**Style Options**:
- `gradient` - Purple gradient background
- `outline` - Bordered box
- `solid` - Solid color

### Service Card

```
[service_card
  title="AI Consulting"
  price="$200/hour"
  icon="💡"
  button_text="Get Started"
  button_url="/contact"
]
Expert guidance on implementing AI solutions for your business
[/service_card]
```

**Where to Use**:
- Services page
- Homepage
- Comparison pages

### Testimonial

```
[testimonial
  author="Sarah Chen"
  role="CTO, TechCorp"
  rating="5"
]
"OpenSourceBox helped us implement ML models that increased our efficiency by 40%. Highly recommended!"
[/testimonial]
```

### Pricing Table

```
[pricing_table]
```

Shows 3-column pricing comparison (Basic, Professional, Enterprise)

**Customize**: Edit in functions.php (line ~680)

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Tip #1: Focus on ONE Revenue Stream First
Don't try to do everything at once. Pick one:
- Consulting, OR
- Courses, OR
- Affiliate marketing

Master it, then add more.

### Tip #2: Email List is Gold
Every subscriber is worth $1-$10/month on average.
- 1,000 subscribers = $1,000-$10,000/month potential
- 10,000 subscribers = $10,000-$100,000/month potential

### Tip #3: Price Based on Value, Not Time
Instead of "$200/hour", sell outcomes:
- "Increase efficiency by 40%: $50,000"
- "Implement chatbot that handles 80% of support: $25,000"

### Tip #4: Content = Marketing
Every blog post is:
- SEO content (Google traffic)
- Social media posts (share snippets)
- Email newsletter content
- Lead magnet material
- Course content foundation

One post = 10+ marketing assets!

### Tip #5: Social Proof is Critical
Add these everywhere:
- Client logos
- Testimonials
- Case studies
- "As seen in" badges
- Social follower counts
- "10,000+ readers"

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake #1: No Clear Call to Action
❌ "Hope they figure out what to do"
✅ Clear CTA on every page: "Book Free Call", "Download Guide"

### Mistake #2: Selling Features Instead of Benefits
❌ "Our AI uses TensorFlow and Python"
✅ "Automate 10 hours of work per week"

### Mistake #3: No Email Follow-Up
❌ People subscribe, never hear from you
✅ Weekly valuable emails, occasional promotion

### Mistake #4: Underpricing
❌ "I'll charge $50/hour to get clients"
✅ "I charge $200/hour because I'm worth it"

### Mistake #5: Analysis Paralysis
❌ Spending months "perfecting" the site
✅ Launch in 2 weeks, improve based on feedback
